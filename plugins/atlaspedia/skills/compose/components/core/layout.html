Why tmp/ is never cleared Design note2 min

The dossier and the plan are what make a resume possible. Clearing them after a build would save little disk space and lose the only record of which source backs which number.

What happens when the search quota runs out

Researchers stop adding sources and list what they could not establish under Could not verify, labelled Not available in sources; nothing is filled in from memory. The count lands in the plan's caveats and in the final report, so you see how much is missing.

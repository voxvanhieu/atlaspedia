Before you move the sliders: with 9 parts of 40 KB and the default 40 KB frame, what will the budget demo say?

  1. It fits — nine short parts stay under 250 KB.
  2. It is over by 150 KB, so atlaspedia would propose the full multi-page site.
  3. It is over, but only the allowance per part changes; the verdict stays "fits".
9 × 40 KB + 40 KB = 400 KB against a 250 KB budget: 150 KB over. The verdict readout turns red and the compose skill would ask whether to switch the topic to a site. Check it on the demo — the "over" preset sets exactly these values.

How to use

Type in the filter box to match a symbol, its meaning or its unit; restrict to symbols or constants with the drop-down; click a column header to sort; the jump bar goes to the first entry of a letter. Entries come from assets/notation.json and from every table.notation and table.constants on the pages — build_site_data.py collects them (run by scaffold.py --sync). A symbol that means two different things on two pages is flagged by the validator: one symbol, one meaning across the site.

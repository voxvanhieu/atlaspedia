Mathematical formulation
$$ s_{\text{part}} = \frac{B - F}{n}, \qquad c = \frac{n_{\text{verified}}}{n_{\text{refs}}} $$
Physical interpretation

A single page has a fixed budget \( B \); the frame \( F \) (hero, summary, glossary, references, trust footer) comes off the top, and the parts share the rest. The coverage \( c \) is the share of a page's references whose Verified badge the dossier earned.

Engineering application

The compose skill writes \( s_{\text{part}} \) into the single-page plan, so the writer knows how much each part may take; the trust footer shows \( c \) as “sources verified n/m” on every page.

Practical implementation

Round \( s_{\text{part}} \) down to whole KB. Count \( n_{\text{verified}} \) from the badge verified spans inside ol.refs, never from the writer's own claim; app.js does this when the page loads.

Running-example instance

Running example: \( B = 250\ \text{KB} \), \( F = 40\ \text{KB} \), \( n = 7 \) parts give \( s_{\text{part}} = 30\ \text{KB} \); a page citing 12 references with 11 verified shows \( c = 11/12 \approx 92\,\% \), both equations as the compose skill and the trust footer define them [4].

$$ S_{\text{site}} = S_{\text{index}} + \sum_{i=1}^{n} S_i, \qquad 30\ \text{KB} \le S_{\text{index}} \le 50\ \text{KB}, \qquad 70\ \text{KB} \le S_i \le 160\ \text{KB} $$

The forward Mercator equations take longitude \( \lambda \) and latitude \( \varphi \) to the plane:

$$ x = R\,(\lambda - \lambda_0), \qquad y = R \ln \tan\!\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\varphi}{2}\right) $$

The inverse recovers the latitude with the Gudermannian function:

$$ \varphi = 2\arctan\!\left(e^{y/R}\right) - \frac{\pi}{2} $$

Equation is what every web-map tile server evaluates; the inverse turns a clicked pixel back into coordinates. Hover an equation and press Copy LaTeX to paste its source into a paper or a notebook.

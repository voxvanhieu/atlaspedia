A brief has 6 parts of 36 KB and the frame takes 40 KB. Does the single page fit the 250 KB budget?

  1. Yes — 6 × 36 KB = 216 KB is under the budget.
  2. No — the frame alone pushes it over.
  3. No — 216 KB + 40 KB = 256 KB is 6 KB over, so the compose skill asks whether to switch to a site.

Exercise 3.1≈ 10 min

A topic in the single format has 7 parts. The page budget is 250 KB, and the frame (hero, summary, glossary, references, trust footer) takes 40 KB. How many KB can each part use? The dossier's build notes ask for about 38 KB per part: does the page fit, and what does atlas-learn do when it does not?

Hint 1

Subtract the frame from the budget first, then share what is left equally between the parts.

Hint 2

The validator does not fail an oversized single page; it warns, and the compose skill turns that warning into a question.

Solution

Per part: \( (250 - 40)\ \text{KB} / 7 = 30\ \text{KB} \). Asked for: \( 7 \times 38\ \text{KB} + 40\ \text{KB} = 306\ \text{KB} \), which is 56 KB over the budget. validate.py warns “single page is 306 KB, over the 250 KB budget”, and atlas-learn asks whether to switch the topic to the full site; the dossier is reused as it is.

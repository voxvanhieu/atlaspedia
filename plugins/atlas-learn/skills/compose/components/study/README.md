# Study — flashcards from the glossary, personal notes, highlights, bookmarks and resume

Use for readers who study the site rather than read it once: a flashcards deck (`flashcards.html`, B4) on the glossary page — the template's `glossary.html` already carries one with every term — or after the section of a module that defines the words the reader must recall; and, once per site, a short "your own notes" callout (`notes.html`, F1) on the overview or a how-to-use section. Notes, highlights, bookmarks and the resume position need no markup at all: the runtime adds them to every page. Snippets: `flashcards.html`, `notes.html`.

Legend — **Use when** tells you when the component earns its place; **Avoid** tells you the common misuse.

## Flashcards — `flashcards.html` (B4)

Shape: `<div class="flashcards" id="fc-…" data-flashcards="all"></div>` or `data-flashcards="Term A, Term B, Term C"` (glossary terms written as in `glossary.json`, matched without regard to case; the deck keeps that order). `data-tags="tag, tag"` limits the deck to entries whose `tags` (a list of strings) or `group` (a string) in `glossary.json` name one of those tags. **Use when** the page introduces vocabulary the reader must recall later (a module's key terms; the whole glossary on the glossary page). **Avoid** more than one deck per page, decks of fewer than 3 cards, and writing definitions into the markup — the cards come from `glossary.json` only, so fix a definition there.

Runtime (`b4-flashcards`, phase `enhance`): a toolbar (due count, a tag filter when the deck's entries carry tags or groups, the direction toggle Term → definition / Definition → term, Shuffle, `CSV ⤓`, `Anki ⤓`), one card at a time (`button.fc-card`: click, Enter or Space flips it; the term side shows `term_local` and `aka`, the definition side `def_local`, from F3), **Again** (key `1`: back to box 1, asked again before the round ends) and **Good** (key `2`: one box up), and five Leitner box bars. Boxes 1–5 are due again after 0, 1, 3, 7 and 14 days; a card never answered is new and due. The records live under `study:cards` in the site's `store` (localStorage, prefixed with `site.json storagePrefix`, so every deck of a site shares them); with storage blocked the deck still works for the visit. When nothing is due the card gives way to "Review every card now". `QC.flashDeck(spec, tags)`, `QC.flashExport(cards, 'csv' | 'anki')` and `QC.leitner(record, ok, now)` are the pure parts.

Exports: **CSV** — `term,definition,term_local,definition_local,tags` (RFC 4180 quoting, tags joined with `; `). **Anki** — a UTF-8 text file with the header lines `#separator:tab`, `#html:true`, `#tags column:3`, then one `front<TAB>back<TAB>tags` line per card (fields HTML-escaped, the second language in `<i>` after a `<br>`, tags space separated with spaces inside a tag turned into `_`); Anki's *File › Import* reads it into the Basic note type (pick "Basic (and reversed card)" for both directions). A real `.apkg` package is out of scope: it is a zipped SQLite database that a static page cannot build without a large library.

## Notes, highlights, bookmarks, resume — `notes.html` (F1)

Runtime only (`f1-notes`, phase `post`), on every page; the snippet is just a callout telling readers about it, with `<button class="btn" data-notes-open>` that opens the panel (hidden when the site opts out).

- **Highlights and notes.** Selecting text inside `main` (mouse, touch or Shift+arrows) shows `div.nt-pop`: three colours (yellow, green, blue), **Note** (a text box; Ctrl+Enter saves) and **Copy link** (the section's URL plus a `#:~:text=` text fragment, so browsers that support it scroll to the exact words). A highlight is stored as its text quote with 32 characters of prefix and suffix (whitespace collapsed) and the id of the nearest `h2`, and is drawn as `mark.hl.hl-1|2|3` around the matching text nodes (the last one gets a ✎ when it has a note). Clicking or pressing Enter on a highlight reopens the popover to recolour, edit the note, copy the link or remove it. Not highlightable: form fields, buttons, SVG, rendered maths and page chrome such as the breadcrumb, the section-meta strip and the flashcards.
- **Orphans.** On load every highlight of the page is searched again (best prefix/suffix match wins); one whose quote is gone is kept, flagged and listed under *Orphaned highlights* in the panel instead of being lost.
- **Bookmarks.** Every `h2` of `main` (not in the hero, demos, cards, quizzes, figures or the trust footer) gets `button.nt-bm` (aria-pressed) after its anchor link.
- **Resume.** The scroll position is saved per page (`study:pos`, throttled, and on leaving the page). Coming back without a `#hash`, more than 20 % down and more than a minute later, a dismissible `div.nt-toast` offers "Resume where you left off: <section>"; scrolling is instant when the reader prefers reduced motion (OS setting or F2).
- **My notes panel.** The top-bar `button.notes-toggle` (or the `m` key, listed in the `?` help) opens `div.nt-panel`: highlights, notes and bookmarks of every page grouped by page (this page first), linked to their section; the orphaned list; **Export JSON** (`{ app: "atlas-learn-notes", version: 1, site, storagePrefix, exported, items }`), **Import JSON** (merges by id, the newer `updated` wins) and **Clear…** (asks for a second click). `QC.notesPanel(open)`, `QC.notesExport()`, `QC.notesImport(data)`, `QC.notesLocate(text, anchor)`, `QC.notesMerge(a, b)`.
- **Storage.** `study:notes` and `study:pos` through the shared `store` (localStorage with try/catch); when storage is blocked everything works for the visit and the panel says so.
- **Single page and print.** Works the same in `Format: single`. Handout mode hides the bookmark buttons and the toast and turns highlights into a quiet grey underline; printing hides every notes control and prints highlights the same quiet way (notes are not printed).
- **Opt out.** `<main data-no-notes>` switches off highlights, bookmarks and resume on that page (the panel stays, listing other pages); `"notes": false` in `site.json` switches the whole feature off. Use the page opt-out for pages whose text is regenerated on every build (an index page) or that are mostly interactive.

## Validator messages this module can trigger

`flashcards fc-x: term "X" is not in glossary.json` · `flashcards fc-x: data-flashcards "…" has an empty item (use "all" or a comma list of terms)` · `flashcards fc-x: data-tags "…" is malformed (a comma list of tag names: …)` · warnings: `flashcards fc-x: tag "x" matches no glossary entry (glossary.json tags / group)` · `flashcards fc-x: the glossary has n terms — a deck needs at least 3` · `data-no-notes on <div> is ignored (put it on <main>)`. Site level: `glossary term "X": tags must be a list of strings` · `glossary term "X": group must be a non-empty string` · `site.json notes must be true or false (got …)`.

How to use

Type in the filter box to match any part of a term, its expansion or its definition; restrict by module with the drop-down; click a column header to sort. Letters without entries are greyed out in the jump bar. When the site carries a second language (site.json secondLanguage), a further column shows each term in it and the filter matches that too.

Flashcards

Practise the same terms one card at a time. Flip a card with Space or a click, then answer 1 Again or 2 Good: a card you know moves up a box and comes back after 1, 3, 7 and then 14 days, a card you missed returns this round. Your progress stays in this browser. CSV and Anki download the deck to study elsewhere.
